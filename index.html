Chapter IV · The intelligence layer

Automate the boring. Judge the rest.

I use generative AI and open-source LLMs to shrink manual, repetitive work, and I work as an OSS compliance analyst, so I know the value of careful, accountable decisions. The skill is knowing when AI helps and when it should not be trusted.

01 · Local-first

Open-source LLMs, Ollama

I pull open-source models with Ollama and integrate them straight into our applications, so useful AI runs where the work already happens.

02 · Focused models

BERT, fine-tuned

When a small specialised model beats a large general one, I fine-tune BERT-family models for the exact language task in front of me.

03 · Compliance

OSS compliance, automated

As an OSS compliance analyst I automate the daily, repeatable parts of the job, and keep people accountable for the calls that matter.
